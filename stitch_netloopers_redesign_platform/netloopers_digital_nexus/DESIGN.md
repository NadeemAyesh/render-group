## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-tier digital marketplace and freelance ecosystem tailored specifically for the MENA tech economy. The brand personality blends institutional reliability with forward-leaning technological optimism: dependable, elite, high-velocity, and human-centric. It bridges elite independent talent (developers, designers, AI engineers, marketers) with enterprise and scaling startup clients.

The visual style merges modern **Glassmorphism** with **Technical Minimalism**:
- Translucent surface layering with precise border strokes.
- Crisp Arabic and Latin typography with rigorous baseline alignment.
- High-contrast visual accents to deliver unmistakable status cues (verified badges, escrow protections, pricing tiers).
- Deep tech slate backgrounds contrasting with vibrant cobalt blue actions and rich golden amber highlights, conveying security, prestige, and transactional trust.
- Native bi-directional layout logic with dedicated first-class RTL (Right-to-Left) optical weighting.

## Colors

The color palette centers on trust, digital mastery, and effortless readability:

- **Primary (`#0B2B60`)**: Deep royal navy anchoring primary brand elements, major section backgrounds, navigational headers, and structural UI cards. Conveys institutional permanence and financial security.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Energetic cobalt blue driving actionable elements, call-to-actions, primary links, focus indicators, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm gold and amber used for high-value highlights, ratings, featured tags, financial balance representations, and verified project badges.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Deep slate for high-legibility body typography, deep boundaries, and subtle iconography.
- **Named Surfaces**:
  - `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`) provides an expansive, clean, low-fatigue background.
  - `surface-glass` combines semi-transparent pure white with backdrop blur for modern layered card compositions.
  - `brand-cyan` (`#0284C7`) and `badge-emerald` (`#10B981`) serve as secondary indicators for availability statuses and real-time escrow safety labels.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes first-class Arabic typographic elegance while ensuring harmonic pairing with Latin scripts:

- **Primary Face (`Cairo`)**: Chosen for its robust architectural proportions, crisp stroke definitions, and native Arabized baseline that stays perfectly legible across display headings, card titles, and body content.
- **Utility & Data Face (`IBM Plex Sans`)**: Used for numerical ratings, prices, metric badges, tags, and micro-labels to provide an engineering precision feel and uniform tabular spacing.
- **RTL Considerations**: 
  - Line height is increased by 10-15% relative to standard Latin types to accommodate Arabic ascenders, descenders, and accent marks without clashing.
  - Tracking/letter-spacing is strictly neutralized (`0` or default) on Arabic text runs to preserve natural cursive stroke connections.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive grid with a strict 4px/8px modular rhythm:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (2rem), maximum container max-width of 1320px, section separation using `margin-lg` (5rem).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem), page margins set to 2rem.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (1rem), page side margins set to `margin` (1.5rem).

### Directional Layout Philosophy
- Pure RTL-first layout structure. Start anchors align to the right, and end anchors align to the left.
- All asymmetrical spacing, such as chevron icons, badge paddings, and avatar offsets, must respect logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent glassmorphic layering, subtle rim lighting, and atmospheric multi-stop drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0)**: Background canvas (`#F8FAFC`) with subtle geometric mesh glows or brand navy gradients (`#0B2B60` to `#071B3B`) for highlight zones.
- **Card Surface (Elevation 1)**: `surface-glass` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) coupled with a 1px border stroke (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`) and ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 43, 96, 0.05)`. Backdrop blur is maintained at `12px`.
- **Interactive Hover (Elevation 2)**: Translates element vertically by `-3px`, elevates ambient shadow to `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(11, 43, 96, 0.12)`, and shifts the border stroke to a glowing translucent secondary blue tint (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.35)`).
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts (Elevation 3)**: Crisp translucent backplates with `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 43, 96, 0.22)`, bounded by a dual-tone edge highlight (`border-t: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` and `border-b: rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The geometric approach embraces fluid modernism with deliberate structure:
- **Base Components**: Inputs, buttons, and individual list items utilize a 0.5rem (8px) corner radius.
- **Cards & Banners**: Service cards, talent cards, and community showcase panels use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- **Hero Containers & Feature Portals**: Main feature callouts use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for architectural presence.
- **Pills & Status Indicators**: Search tags, category filters, and live availability chips use continuous full pill styling (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for optimal tap targets and touch aesthetics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#2563EB` fill with white typography, crisp 0.5rem corner radius, and subtle bottom inner highlight. On hover, transitions to `#1D4ED8` with a diffused blue glow (`0 8px 16px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Translucent glass background with 1px border (`#0B2B60` or `#E2E8F0`), text in primary navy. Hover fills with light cobalt wash (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`).
- **Hero CTA**: Amber accent fill (`#F59E0B`) with high-contrast navy text (`#071B3B`) for critical conversion paths.

### Service & Digital Product Cards
- **Structure**: Aspect ratio thumbnail on top, category chip floating top-end, creator profile avatar overlapping the media baseline.
- **Price Block**: Clear, transparent baseline container showing "Starts at" in `label-sm` slate, followed by large tabular figures in `headline-sm` with currency unit.
- **Rating**: Tertiary amber star with numeric rating and count formatted in `label-md`.

### Category Pills & Filter Chips
- Horizontal scrolling pill rail with native RTL momentum scrolling.
- Inactive pills: White glass surface, border `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.9)`, body slate text.
- Active pills: Solid `#0B2B60` fill with white text, or solid `#2563EB` fill with accompanying counter badge.

### Inputs & Global Search Bar
- Search container elevated to hero status: large 56px height, rounded pill shape, right-aligned search glyph in RTL, embedded quick-filter dropdown, and immediate full-width submit button.
- Subtle inner shadow with a focus ring of 3px soft cobalt (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`).

### Talent & Freelancer Profiles
- Circular avatar framed by a dynamic badge (e.g., green active pulse dot for online status, golden star ring for top-rated).
- Skills tags rendered as compact, low-saturation soft badges (`#F1F5F9`).
- Action footer featuring instant portfolio preview button and direct proposal hire trigger.

### Community & Trust Banner
- Deep navy gradient block (`#0B2B60` to `#071B3B`) featuring stacked circular user avatars, active project stats with bold metric counters, and verified escrow badges.
